Privacy policy

Crazy Downloader 2.1.0 and its companion browser extension process all downloads, URLs, and preferences locally on your computer. We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.

1. Zero external telemetry or tracking

Neither the Windows desktop application nor the browser extension includes analytics SDKs, crash telemetry reporters, or advertising trackers. Your browsing history, download URLs, and saved filenames never leave your device.

2. Where local data is stored on your machine

Application preferences (crazy-settings.json) and your local download list (crazy-downloads.json) are stored exclusively in your Windows user profile directory at %APPDATA%\crazy-downloader-desktop. You can clear or delete these files at any time.

3. How browser extension permissions are used

The companion extension requests downloads, webRequest, contextMenus, storage, tabs, and nativeMessaging solely to hand user initiated downloads and detected video streams to the local desktop process over http://127.0.0.1:49820 or the local Windows Native Messaging host (com.crazy.downloader).

4. Direct network connections

Crazy Downloader 2.1.0 only makes outbound network requests to the exact file or video URLs you choose to download, and to the update metadata URL if you configure or run an update check.

5. Privacy questions and contact

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out at crazydownloadersport@gmail.com.
